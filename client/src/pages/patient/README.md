# Patient & Longitudinal Health Record Module (`client/src/pages/patient`)

This directory represents the longitudinal health record views anchored by Ayushman Bharat Health Account (ABHA) IDs.

## Key Screens & Features
- **Longitudinal Health Record**: Complete historical audit trail of frontline home visits, vitals snapshots, triage classifications, facility referrals, and doctor consultation orders.
- **Village Population Registry**: Searchable database of enrolled village residents and their active continuity statuses.
